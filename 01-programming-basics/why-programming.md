# Why programming?

Programming is the process of giving a computer precise instructions to carry out a task. Computers can perform those instructions very quickly and repeat them consistently, which makes programming useful for everything from calculating a budget to running a communication network.

Programs also let people turn ideas into tools. A recipe app organizes steps and ingredients; a map app combines location data and routes; a game updates a world in response to a player's actions. In each case, a programmer describes the rules and the computer follows them.

Programming is not only typing code. It includes understanding a problem, breaking it into manageable steps, testing possible solutions, and improving them when something goes wrong. You do not need to understand everything at once: small experiments are a good way to learn.

## Try thinking like a programmer

Describe how you would teach someone to make a cup of tea. Notice that vague steps such as "make it hot" need to become clear actions. Turning an everyday process into precise steps is a first taste of programming.
