# Encryption

Encryption transforms readable information (plaintext) into a form that is difficult to understand (ciphertext) without the right key. It helps protect information when stored or sent across networks.

In **symmetric encryption**, the same secret key is used to encrypt and decrypt data. In **public-key encryption**, a public key and a related private key have different roles. Secure communication protocols such as TLS combine cryptographic techniques to establish keys and protect a connection.

Encryption depends on protecting keys and choosing well-reviewed algorithms and libraries. Making your own encryption scheme or hiding data with a home-grown method is rarely safe.
